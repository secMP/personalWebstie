I am currently a Postdoctoral Researcher at Karlstad University, where I continue to explore the intersection of human-computer interaction and usable privacy in the context of cross-platform tracking. This work is part of the >EU Horizon's Real-PETS project.


In my PhD research, I focused on human-computer interaction and usable security exploring how secure technologies, user preferences, device configurations shape user privacy in smart spaces deployed with IoT systems. Advised by Dr. Adam Lee.

Research

My work focuses on understanding how the introduction of emerging privacy-preserving technologies, such as Trusted Execution Environments — impacts users' mental models and privacy norms in domestic settings. I also investigate effective mechanisms for user awareness in pervasive sensing environments, moving beyond notification-heavy paradigms toward more intuitive, context-sensitive designs.

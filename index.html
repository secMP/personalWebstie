About Me

I am a Ph.D. student at the University of Pittsburgh in the Department of Computer Science. I am currently working with Dr. Adam Lee.

My research focuses on human-computer interaction (HCI) and usable security. I mainly look into the impacts of introducing secure technologies on user's privacy. Furthermore, my research centralizes effective ways to provide user awareness on space sensing, as the current system relies heavily on notifications.
